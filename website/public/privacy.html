Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Toveli ("we," "our," or "us") is an AI-powered travel planning app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

We believe in keeping things simple: we collect only what we need to make the app work, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you with ads or analytics.

1. Information We Collect

Location data

Toveli requests location access only when you use the Explore feature to find places near you. Your location is used on-demand to display nearby results and is not stored on our servers or tracked over time. You can deny location access and the rest of the app works normally.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not serve ads or use ad tracking identifiers
  • We do not use cookies (this is a mobile app)
  • We do not collect behavioral analytics or usage patterns
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Toveli app and its features
  • Generate and personalize AI trip recommendations
  • Sync your trips, preferences, and memories across your devices
  • Send push notifications you've opted into (trip alerts, booking reminders, daily briefings)
  • Process subscriptions and manage your plan
  • Respond to support requests

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored using Supabase, a cloud database platform. Key protections include:

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Database access is controlled by row-level security policies, so users can only access their own data
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS
  • Gmail OAuth tokens are stored securely and deleted when you disconnect or delete your account

Some data is also stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage for offline access. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you delete your account.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Toveli:

  • Supabase -- database, authentication, and server-side functions
  • Expo -- app framework and push notification delivery
  • Google Maps -- map display and place information
  • Apple App Store / Google Play -- subscription processing
  • AI services -- trip generation and travel assistance (your data is sent to AI providers to generate recommendations; it is not used to train AI models)

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5. Push Notifications

Toveli can send push notifications for trip alerts, booking reminders, departure countdowns, and daily briefings. All notification types can be individually toggled in Settings. You can disable all notifications through your device settings at any time. Notification preferences are stored locally on your device.

6. Gmail Integration

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, Toveli accesses your email to find booking confirmations (flights, hotels, etc.) and import them into your trips. We store an OAuth refresh token to maintain this connection. We do not read emails beyond booking-related messages. You can disconnect Gmail at any time, which deletes the stored tokens. Tokens are also deleted if you delete your account.

7. Your Rights

For all users

  • Access your data: You can view all your data within the app (trips, preferences, memories, chat history)
  • Export your data: Use the "Export my data" option in Settings > Personalization & privacy to download all your data as JSON
  • Delete specific data: Clear chat history, trip memory, or individual memories from Settings
  • Delete your account: Use Settings > Account > Delete account to permanently delete all your data from our servers and your device
  • Control personalization: Pause or resume travel memory learning at any time in Settings
  • Control notifications: Toggle each notification type individually in Settings

For users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right to access (Article 15) -- View and export your data through the app
  • Right to rectification (Article 16) -- Edit your profile and trip data at any time
  • Right to erasure (Article 17) -- Delete your account and all associated data
  • Right to data portability (Article 20) -- Export your data via the in-app export feature
  • Right to object (Article 21) -- Disable personalization learning at any time

Our legal basis for processing your data is contract performance (providing the app's services) and your consent (for optional features like location access, notifications, and Gmail integration).

For users in California (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Children's Privacy

Toveli is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed from our servers and your device. We do not retain backups of deleted account data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision.
